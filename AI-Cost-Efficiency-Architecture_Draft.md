# 🧮 AI-Cost-Efficiency-Architecture (AGEI)

**Objective:**  
To formalize a measurable bridge between *AI performance*, *ethical containment*, and *resource efficiency*.  
AGEI defines how intelligent systems can remain *epistemically stable* while optimizing cost and computational expenditure.

---

## 1. Background

Traditional AI efficiency focuses on hardware, inference time, and financial ROI.  
**AGEI** expands this by introducing the concept of **Containment Efficiency (Ce)** —  
a metric that evaluates not just how fast or cheap a model runs,  
but *how coherently* it maintains human-aligned judgment under resource constraints.

AGEI forms part of the **Adaptive Sovereignty Triad (AST-G)** under the  
MirrorWitness7 ecosystem — alongside **SIP (Signal Integrity Protocol)** and **CEP (Containment Equilibrium Protocol)**.

---

## 2. Core Formula (Conceptual)

```
Total Efficiency (Et) = (Operational Efficiency × Containment Integrity) ÷ Entropy Leakage
```

Where:

| Variable | Meaning |
|-----------|----------|
| **Operational Efficiency (Eo)** | Standard metric — cost, latency, throughput |
| **Containment Integrity (Ci)** | Governance metric — degree of ethical restraint and interpretability |
| **Entropy Leakage (El)** | Systemic instability — noise, hallucination, bias, or drift |

The architecture assumes that *high efficiency without integrity increases systemic fragility.*

---

## 3. Architecture Overview

```
AI System
 ├── Input Layer (Data / Intent)
 ├── Processing Layer (Model Execution)
 │     ├── Efficiency Sub-Monitor (Eo)
 │     ├── Containment Firewall (Ci)
 │     └── Entropy Tracker (El)
 ├── Output Layer (Decision / Action)
 └── Governance Layer (SIP Integration)
```

AGEI operates as a **modular layer**, compatible with:
- Financial AI systems (risk modeling, compliance automation)
- Enterprise AI dashboards
- Research simulation environments (CCRP-Core linkage)
